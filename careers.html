About the role

You will be the daily point of contact for three to five trucks. Each morning you check where the drivers are, what is on the board and which brokers are worth a call. By the end of the day every truck has a plan for tomorrow and every rate confirmation is filed.

What you will do

  • Search load boards and broker contacts for dry van and reefer freight in the driver's lane.
  • Negotiate rates and detention terms, then lock in the rate confirmation.
  • Build weekly plans that balance miles, home time and revenue per truck.
  • Keep drivers, brokers and shippers updated on pickup and delivery times.
  • Escalate breakdowns, delays and paperwork issues to the right team fast.

What we look for

  • At least one year in dispatch, brokerage or trucking operations.
  • Confident English on the phone and clear written updates.
  • Comfort with load boards, spreadsheets and a busy inbox.
  • Calm under pressure and honest when something goes wrong.

What we offer

Base pay plus a percentage of gross on every truck you run, a paid three-week onboarding, equipment and software provided, and a team lead available on every shift.
