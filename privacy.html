Privacy Policy

At DispatchYou we take the privacy of our website visitors seriously. This page sets out the categories of information the site gathers and stores, and the ways in which that information is used.

Questions about anything in this policy, or requests for further detail, can be sent to office@dispatchyou.com.

The scope of this policy is limited to what happens online: it covers people who visit this website and the information they provide to us, or that the site collects, while they are here. Information obtained offline, or through any channel other than this website, is not covered.

Consent

Using this website signals that you accept this policy and the terms it contains.

Information we collect

Each time the site asks for personal details, the purpose of the request will be explained at the point where the details are requested.

When you get in touch with us directly, we may also learn your name, your email address, your telephone number, what your message says, any files attached to it, and anything else you decide to tell us.

If you ask for a quote or apply for a job, we may collect contact details such as your name, the name of your company, a postal address, an email address and a phone number.

How we use your information

The information gathered on this site serves several purposes, among them:

  • Running, supporting and maintaining the website.
  • Making the website better, more personal and broader in scope.
  • Learning and analyzing the ways visitors interact with the website.
  • Creating new products, services and features.
  • Getting in touch with you, either ourselves or via a partner, for support, for updates and news about the website, and for marketing or promotional messages.
  • Sending email, and detecting and preventing fraudulent activity.

SMS Communication Policy

Your mobile number, and the fact that you agreed to receive text messages, will never be passed to third parties or affiliated companies so they can market to you. Any data collected as part of your SMS opt-in is used solely to send the messages you signed up for.

When you give us your mobile number and opt in, you are consenting to receive texts of the following kinds:

  • Notifications about loads, reminders of appointments and alerts about our service.
  • Answers to questions you have sent us and notices concerning your account.

How often messages arrive will vary. Your carrier may charge for messages and data. You can text STOP whenever you like to unsubscribe, or HELP if you need support.

You are never required to accept text messages in order to purchase anything from us.

Log Files

In common with most websites, this site keeps server log files. Each record may contain the visitor’s IP address, the browser used, the internet provider, a date and time stamp, the page arrived from and the page left to, and occasionally a count of clicks. Nothing in these logs is connected to details that could identify a specific person. The data helps us spot trends, run the site, understand how people navigate around it and build up a general demographic picture.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Cookies are used on this website to remember things like the choices a visitor has made and the pages they have looked at. With that information the site can adapt what it shows to your browser type and similar factors, giving you a smoother experience. A full description of each cookie appears in our cookie policy.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Among the third-party providers on this site is Google, which relies on a cookie called DART to serve advertisements that reflect a visitor’s activity on this website and elsewhere on the internet. If you prefer not to have the DART cookie set, you can opt out via the settings page of the Google advertising and content network.

Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Every advertising partner we work with maintains a separate privacy policy of its own; consult those documents to learn how each partner treats data.

External ad servers and networks embed technologies (cookies, JavaScript, web beacons and the like) in the adverts and links shown on this website; these are delivered directly to your browser, and in doing so the provider automatically learns your IP address. Such technologies allow the providers to gauge how effective their advertising is and to tailor the adverts you encounter on websites you visit.

Please be aware that DispatchYou can neither see nor control the cookies set by outside advertisers.

Third Party Privacy Policies

This document covers only DispatchYou; it does not extend to other advertisers or websites. For more information, including the ways to opt out, please refer to the individual privacy policies published by those external ad servers.

Another option is to turn cookies off in your browser settings; step-by-step guidance for doing this is provided on the website of each browser maker.

CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Residents of California enjoy a number of rights under the CCPA, including the right to:

  • Require a business that gathers personal data to reveal both the categories and the individual items of personal data it holds about them.
  • Require a business to erase the personal data it holds about them.
  • Direct a business that sells personal data to stop selling theirs.

Requests of this kind are answered within one month. To use any of these rights, simply get in touch.

GDPR Data Protection Rights

It matters to us that you are fully aware of the rights you hold over your data. Each visitor has the following rights:

  • The right to access – you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you; a modest fee may be charged.
  • The right to rectification – you can require us to fix details you consider wrong, or to fill in details you consider missing.
  • The right to erasure – in certain circumstances you can require us to delete your personal data.
  • The right to restrict processing – in certain circumstances you can require us to curtail the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to object to processing – in certain circumstances you can oppose the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability – in certain circumstances you can require us to send the data we hold either to you or to another organization.

We respond to such requests within one month. If you wish to exercise one of these rights, please reach out to us.

Children’s Information

Keeping children safe on the internet is something we care about. Parents and guardians are encouraged to observe, join in with and direct what their children do online.

DispatchYou never knowingly gathers personal details from anyone under the age of 13. Should you suspect that a child in your care has submitted such details through this website, let us know immediately and we will make every effort to erase the information from our systems without delay.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be revised now and then, so please revisit this page periodically to see the current version. Any changes become effective the moment they are published here.
