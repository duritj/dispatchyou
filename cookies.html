Here DispatchYou describes the cookies this website relies on and the options you have for managing them. Read it together with our privacy policy and our terms and conditions.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your computer or phone; nearly every professional website uses them, and so does this one, to make your visit work better. Below we set out what these files collect, what we do with that data and the reasons we occasionally have to keep them.

You will also find instructions for blocking cookies, bearing in mind that blocking them can reduce or disable certain functions of the website.

How We Use Cookies

The purposes for which this site sets cookies are described on this page. Sadly, for most cookies there is no accepted method of turning them off individually while keeping the functionality they power, so if you are not certain whether a given cookie is required, our advice is to keep cookies switched on.

Disabling Cookies

Every browser has a setting that blocks cookies; consult your browser’s help pages to find it. Be aware, though, that turning cookies off changes how this website (and a great many others) behaves, and typically means certain features stop working. We therefore suggest leaving cookies on.

The Cookies We Set

Depending on which parts of the website you use, the cookies listed here may be stored:

  • Account cookies. Should you open an account with us, cookies are used during registration and for routine account management. Most are cleared when you sign out, although a few may persist so your settings are remembered on your return.
  • Newsletter cookies. If you sign up for our email newsletter, a cookie may note that you are already subscribed and control whether particular messages, intended only for subscribers, are displayed to you.
  • Form cookies. After you submit a form, for instance the contact form or the quote request, a cookie may keep your details so we can correspond with you later.
  • Performance cookies. These gather anonymous technical measurements, for example page load times, which help us keep the website quick and dependable.

Third Party Cookies

Certain cookies on this website are supplied by outside companies we trust. The list below describes the external cookies you might come across here.

  • Analytics. A popular analytics tool is used on this website so that we can learn how people use it and where it could be improved; its cookies may record details such as the length of your visit and the pages you looked at.
  • Feature testing. Occasionally we trial new functions and small design changes. During such trials, cookies ensure that you keep seeing the same version of the site and tell us which of the changes visitors respond to best.
  • Conversion tracking. Advertising helps pay for this website. These cookies identify visitors who reached us by clicking an advert, so that we can check our adverts are shown to people with a real interest in our services.
  • Advertising networks. Networks that deliver adverts may set cookies of their own to choose relevant adverts and to cap the number of times you see any one of them.
  • Social media. Share buttons and plugins from social networks are present on this website. To function, those networks may place cookies via our pages, which they may then use to add to your profile or to the information they keep for their own purposes.

More Information

We trust this page has answered your questions. To repeat the point made earlier: when in doubt about a cookie, keeping it enabled is generally the safer choice, since it may be tied to a function you rely on.
