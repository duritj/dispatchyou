How to get more out of your dry van

Dry van dispatch is built for carriers and owner-operators running enclosed 53-foot trailers. Dry vans carry freight that needs no temperature control or special handling: packaged consumer goods, furniture, appliances, electronics and pallets of just about anything.

Our job is to keep those trailers moving. That means finding loads for empty trucks, lining up pickup and delivery windows and staying in touch with drivers so the whole day runs without surprises.

Brokers, logistics firms and larger fleets all offer some form of dry van dispatch. For a small carrier or a one-truck operation, handing it to a dedicated team is often the difference between chasing freight and actually hauling it.

We treat your trucks like our own: consistent lanes, rates negotiated upward and paperwork finished the same day.
